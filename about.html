The Craft Origin

It All Started with a
Copper Kettle.

In 2018, our founder Leo launched a quiet revolution to free soda from artificial high-fructose corn syrup and synthetic flavors. Armed with copper kettles and whole natural botanicals, he revived authentic small-batch soda making.

What began as an obsession with true flavor blossomed into a movement. We don't bottle mass-produced concentrate; we slow-simmer whole fruits, spices, and roots. Every batch of Craft Soda is a testament to artisanal patience and genuine ingredients.

Our Brewing Philosophy

01

Pure Botanicals

We only use real spices, fresh-pressed ginger root, whole citrus peels, and natural vanilla beans.

02

Slow Simmering

Our kettle process gently simmers botanicals, extracting rich essential oils and caramelizing cane sugar naturally.

03

Glass Bottles

Craft soda belongs in glass. We package in recyclable glass bottles to preserve crisp effervescence and pristine flavor.

The Golden Ratio

The Master Craft

01. Temperature Sync

Careful kettle heat management ensures delicate citrus and herbal aromatics never scorch.

02. Slow Infusion

Triple-steeped botanicals deliver a depth of flavor that commercial factory sodas cannot achieve.

03. Pure Clarity

Natural cold-settling preserves the aromatic essential oils without cloudy artificial additives.

04. Velvet Micro-Fizz

Chilled micro-carbonation delivers smooth, tight bubbles that dance on the tongue with zero bite.
